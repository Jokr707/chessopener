Privacy policy

ChessOpener does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app works entirely offline. It makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Your training progress and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The Stockfish chess engine used by the Analysis board runs on your device.

This website has no cookies, analytics or third-party content. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see the GitHub privacy statement.
